## Live pipeline deals (Q2 2026 snapshot) - - playbook applied in real negotiations

### Aleena Vigoda - - Anything.com (Cultural Economist)
- **Stage:** Proposal sent, awaiting confirmation
- **How the deal opened:** Aleena reached out first after seeing the Replit case study - - proof that citing named case studies in editorial/organic content generates inbound
- **Angle used:** vibe-coding angle, two discovery calls before the proposal
- **Negotiation point:** she initially proposed 100% first-month / 50% recurring affiliate revenue share; Alex countered with 30% lifetime revenue share + a 20% discount code (AICENTRAL20) - - trading a lower ongoing percentage for a permanent (lifetime) arrangement plus a customer-facing incentive
- **Signal to watch:** she mentioned running an affiliate session soon and in-person events - - both expansion opportunities beyond the initial deal

### Luís Girão - - UX Pilot (reports to CEO Adam Fard)
- **Stage:** Negotiating - - budget/CPC objection
- **Objection:** full package felt too expensive; a previous $325/newsletter placement elsewhere had poor CPC
- **Alex's reframe:** 1 LinkedIn carousel + 1 CEO interview (with Adam Fard) at the same $325 price point
- **Justification used:** native content investment framing (content lives permanently on the AI Central site, 20k visits/month) + explicit math - - ~$16 CPM / ~$1.60 CPC for the offer vs. LinkedIn Ads benchmarks of $30-80+ CPM / $5-12+ CPC
- **Why this matters for training:** this is the clearest example on file of turning a "too expensive" objection into a same-price, higher-value reframe backed by real math - - see `04_objections_playbook.md` for the full breakdown

### Claire Chen - - Dokie AI (Marketing & PR Manager)
- **Stage:** Proposal sent, awaiting review
- **How the deal opened:** initial outreach referenced Dokie's existing sponsored post with Sabahudin Murtic - - relevant, specific social proof of what worked for them before
- **Buying signal:** Claire asked about rates immediately - - a strong early signal worth accelerating on
- **Positioning:** Dokie framed as a PPT-native AI tool that complements Gamma rather than competing with it - - useful pattern for any prospect adjacent to an existing partner
