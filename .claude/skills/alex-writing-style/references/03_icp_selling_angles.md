# AI Central - - ICP & Selling Angles

## Ideal Customer Profile

| Fit | Company type | Job title | Price range | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Good ✅** | B2C Productivity SaaS, AI Agents/Assistants, CRMs | Founder/CEO/CMO, Head/VP/Director of Marketing, Partnership Manager, Head of Growth | Free tier available or under $39/mo | Brand awareness, product/feature launch |
| **Medium** | - | Affiliate Marketing Manager, Digital Marketing, Head of Demand Gen | $39 - $99/mo | Lead gen only |
| **Bad ❌** | Large B2B corporations (e.g. Microsoft, Oracle), Financial Institutions, IT & Cybersecurity | - | Over $99/mo, no free tier | Lead gen only with cost cap |

**Rule of thumb:** the closer a prospect is to "Good," the more effort (custom carousels, interview offers, multi-touch bundles) is worth investing before the first call. "Bad" fits should be politely deprioritized or redirected to a smaller, cheaper test placement only if they insist

## Selling angles - - match the angle to what the prospect is doing

| Prospect scenario | Angle to use |
| --- | --- |
| They run sponsored newsletters already | Better rates + better creative + bigger bundle |
| They are launching a feature or product | Launch wave with multi-touch across LinkedIn + Beehiiv |
| They have a lead magnet or report | High-intent traffic and email capture |
| They sell a course or cohort | Deadline-driven pushes with proof and urgency |
| They are early-stage and need credibility | Borrowed trust with senior operators (name-drop ElevenLabs, Replit, Gamma, HubSpot, Notion) |

## Qualifying questions to ask early

- What's the primary goal: signups, demos, or awareness? (determines package - - see objection "Send pricing")
- Is there a free tier or entry price point, and what does it cost per month? (ICP fit check)
- Do they already run sponsored newsletter placements elsewhere? What did they pay, what was the CPC/CPM? (pricing anchor + angle)
- Is there a launch, feature drop, or deadline-driven moment to align to? (urgency + angle)
- Who owns the budget - - founder/CMO directly, or a marketing manager who needs to get sign-off? (deal velocity read)
