LinkedIn Ads benchmarks typically run **$30-80+ CPM** and **$5-12+ CPC**. AI Central's guaranteed-minimum CPMs/CPCs across every package above are lower, and the content itself becomes a durable, republishable asset - - unlike a paid ad that stops the moment spend stops
