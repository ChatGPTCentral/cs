# AI Central - - Objections Playbook

Every documented objection and Alex's exact reply framework. Always reply in Alex's voice (see `05_voice_tone_style_guide.md`) - - no em dashes, no terminal periods, "Cheers, A" sign-off

## "We are not ready"

> Totally fair - - want a quick 15 min to map your next product moment and I'll suggest 2 package options that fit the timeline?

**Why it works:** doesn't push for a close now, reframes the ask as free strategic value (mapping their moment), and offers optionality (2 packages) instead of a single hard pitch

## "Send pricing"

> Yep - - quick context first: are you optimizing for signups, demos, or awareness? I'll send the right option so we don't spam you with irrelevant packages

**Why it works:** resists the urge to blast a price sheet; qualifies the goal first so the eventual price feels tailored, not generic; signals competence ("we won't spam you")

## "Too expensive"

> Got it - - if budget is tight, we can start with 1 placement, measure, then roll winners into a bundle - - what KPI matters most for you this month?

**Why it works:** de-risks the ask by shrinking commitment to a single placement, implies a natural upsell path once it performs, and redirects to a KPI conversation instead of arguing about price

## "We do this in-house"

> Love that - - we plug in where it's hardest: distribution to senior operators plus editorial creative that performs in feed - - we can also use your assets and just handle placement

**Why it works:** validates their existing capability instead of competing with it, isolates the actual value-add (distribution + editorial performance), and offers a lighter-touch "just placement" option to lower the barrier to yes

## Real-world negotiation example: budget/CPC objection (Luís Girão, UX Pilot - - live pipeline deal)

**Objection:** the full package felt too expensive, and a previous $325/newsletter placement elsewhere delivered poor CPC

**Alex's counter:** reframed the offer into 1 LinkedIn carousel + 1 CEO interview (with the CEO, Adam Fard) at the same $325 price point, then justified it with:
- Native content investment framing - - the content lives permanently on the AI Central website (20k visits/month), unlike a one-off newsletter mention
- Explicit CPM/CPC math for the offer (~$16 CPM, ~$1.60 CPC) benchmarked against LinkedIn Ads (~$30-80+ CPM, ~$5-12+ CPC)
- A credibility/brand-asset angle - - an interview with the CEO is a repurposable asset, not just a media buy

**Takeaway for the agent:** when a prospect anchors on a bad past CPC, don't argue the number - - reframe the format (add an interview or evergreen asset), then out-math the objection with real CPM/CPC comparisons against paid social benchmarks

## General negotiation principles (from the writing style guide)

- **Acknowledge positive intent first** - - "thanks for getting back to me," "I really appreciate your transparency on the budget side"
- **Raise the concern clearly**, often as numbered points
- **Bring evidence** - - CPM/CPC math, case study results, subscriber/engagement numbers
- **Propose a specific alternative**, framed as win-win: "In the spirit of a win-win collaboration, [proposed alternative]"
- **Keep the door open** - - never let a negotiation email end on a hard no or an ultimatum
