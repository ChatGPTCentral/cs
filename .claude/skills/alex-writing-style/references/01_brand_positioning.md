# AI Central - - Brand & Positioning

## Who we are

**AI Central Media** (legal entity: AI Central S.R.L., Italian-registered) is a media and e-learning company at seed stage, founded and led by **Alex Fiore** (CEO & Founder). Alex holds an MSc in Quantitative Finance, previously led go-to-market at a UK AI fintech, teaches AI & Monetization at Cozora Academy, and is a founding member of the SF-based AI Collective

**Mission:** Help senior professionals use AI to work smarter - - through editorial content, practical tutorials, C-level interviews, and sponsored partnerships with the best AI and SaaS tools in the market

**Positioning statement:**
> AI Central is a media group and flagship publication focused on practical AI for senior professionals. We turn attention into pipeline for AI and SaaS brands by pairing premium placements with editorial-grade creative that speaks to senior operators

We are not a blog. We are not an agency. We are a trusted media brand with a curated, senior audience - - the credibility of a trade publication with the speed and agility of a startup

**Aspirational comp (internal, confirmed by Alex 4 Sep 2026):** imagine Bloomberg Businessweek, but for AI. This is the caliber and editorial authority AI Central is building toward - use it to calibrate tone and ambition, not as a literal claim of affiliation with or endorsement by Bloomberg. When it appears in outward-facing copy (decks, landing pages, pitches), phrase it as an invitation to imagine ("imagine X, but for Y"), never as a direct equivalence ("we are the X of Y") or an implied partnership

## Audience (who reads us / who our sponsors reach)

- **Job titles:** Founders, C-Suite, VPs, Directors, Senior Managers, consultants, and knowledge workers who manage teams or budgets
- **Age range:** 35 - 55
- **Geography:** North America (primary), Europe (secondary)
- **Seniority breakdown:** ~40% Founders / C-level / Executives
- **Top industries:** Consulting, SaaS, Education (also Finance, Marketing)

**What they want:** practical, actionable AI guidance they can apply this week; to look smart and ahead of the curve in front of their teams; tool recommendations from a trusted editorial voice, not ads disguised as content; concise formats readable in under 5 minutes

**What they hate:** hype without substance, "AI will take your job" takes, listicles with no depth, being talked down to

## Content pillars

All editorial output (including sponsored content) maps to one of these seven pillars - - always anchor sponsored work to at least one so it reads as editorial, not as an ad:

1. AI Tool Reviews & Tutorials
2. Workflow & Productivity
3. Leadership & Strategy
4. Industry Analysis
5. C-Level Interviews
6. Sponsored Deep Dives (partner-funded editorial that meets our standards)
7. Prompt & Skill Building

## Sponsored content standards

1. **Real value first** - - content must be genuinely useful independent of the sponsor; the product is the natural solution, not the entire subject
2. **Honest positioning** - - never claim a product does things it doesn't; enthusiastic, not misleading
3. **Editorial voice** - - sponsored carousels/newsletter sections are written in our voice, not the client's marketing copy
4. **Disclosure** - - always disclosed ("Sponsored by [Partner]" / "In partnership with [Partner]")
5. **Fit check** - - verify against the ICP before accepting (see `03_icp_selling_angles.md`)

## What AI Central is NOT (clarify if a prospect has the wrong model)

- **Not a marketing agency** - - we don't manage campaigns or run paid media for clients; we produce editorial-grade content and distribute it to our owned audience
- **Not a freelance writing service** - - placements are tied to our distribution; you're buying reach + creative together
- **Not a tech blog** - - we don't review every tool that launches; we curate for our specific senior audience
- **Not a B2B lead gen firm** - - we drive awareness, education, and intent; direct-response results are a bonus, not the core promise
- **Not focused on beginners** - - our readers know what ChatGPT is; we don't explain basics
