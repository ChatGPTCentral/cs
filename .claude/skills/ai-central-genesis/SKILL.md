# AI Central: the ultimate truth

This is the one canonical account of AI Central's real history. It exists because
Alex is the only person who knows what actually happened, including offline -
no amount of email archaeology alone can resolve identity, intent or outcome.
Every fact below is either **verified** (found in a real email, calendar entry
or document) or **per Alex** (his own direct account, not otherwise provable
from the mailbox). Never blend the two silently - the source tag stays on the
fact wherever it is repeated

**This file is a summary, not a replacement for the detail.** The full
evidence for every claim below lives in
`.claude/skills/inbox-ledger/ledger/stories/*.md` - one file per person,
project or relationship, each with its own threads, dates and sourcing. This
file exists to give the connected arc that no single story file can show on
its own

## How this file stays true

- **Do not infer a fact and write it here as settled.** A gap in the record
  stays a gap, marked as such, until Alex confirms it directly - the same
  rule the project already applies to `needs-from-alex` fields in playbooks
- **Every new correction from Alex gets written in immediately**, not queued.
  When Alex corrects something here or in a story file, this file updates in
  the same pass
- **Cite the story file** for any claim that has one, so the detail is one
  click away
- **Open questions are listed explicitly** at the end, not hidden by silence

## The arc, in order

### Before AI Central had a name (2023)

The earliest evidence in the whole corpus is a calendar entry, not an email:
"Mats + Alex," 27 May 2023 - Alex tried to sell Mats something and it did not
work out. *Per Alex, verified only as a calendar entry - see mats-outreach.md*

Through the second half of 2023, Alex ran early sales and partnership
attempts under the "ChatGPT Central" name, working the pattern of a solo
operator with a growing newsletter audience:

- Pitched to become an "Ambassador" for AuthoredUp, a LinkedIn writing tool,
  offering the newsletter's then-17K-follower community. Declined. *Verified
  - see authoredup-ambassador.md*
- Ran outreach with Fahed Bizzari / The ChatGPT Accelerator, September 2023
  through February 2024. Closed, but Alex flagged it as worth revisiting.
  *Per Alex - see fahed-bizzari-chatgpt-accelerator.md*
- Peter Bittner pitched himself as a consultant for AI Central, December
  2023. Nothing came of it. *Per Alex - see peter-bittner-consulting-pitch.md*

### Building the first product (Oct 2023 - Feb 2024)

Alex, Alessandra Serinelli and Matteo Brunati built an early version of what
became the AI Central library together - "ci lavoravamo tutti e 3." The
project concluded; both Alessandra and Matteo are no longer part of AI
Central. This is a different, earlier build than today's Ultimate AI Library
product - do not conflate the two when writing about the library's history.
*Per Alex - see ai-library-early-version.md*

### GrowthSchool: the longest-running partner relationship (Feb 2024 - present)

GrowthSchool first appears 23 Feb 2024 (Vikas Kumar, a campaign inquiry), goes
quiet, gets an independent second cold pitch in Jan 2025 (dhrithi jain), then
becomes a real paying deal by Jun 2025 - by then "ChatGPT Central" is
operating as theaicentral.net, and the point of contact is Sam, who is Sunny
Singh (see below). *Body-verified from real email content - see
growthschool-sponsorship.md*

Per Alex, in confidence: GrowthSchool was plausibly AI Central's best client
in 2025. Something broke in Q4 2025 and they stopped paying. They came back
at some point since AI Central still owed them unrun ads. Difficult to deal
with in general, but they pay - worth tolerating, and worth pushing hard on
again, since Q4 2026 could mean real money.
