# AI Central: the ultimate truth

This is the one canonical account of AI Central's real history. It exists because
Alex is the only person who knows what actually happened, including offline -
no amount of email archaeology alone can resolve identity, intent or outcome.
Every fact below is either **verified** (found in a real email, calendar entry
or document) or **per Alex** (his own direct account, not otherwise provable
from the mailbox). Never blend the two silently - the source tag stays on the
fact wherever it is repeated

**This file summarizes ~65 relationships and events, not the whole ledger.**
Alex told this session the real story of AI Central across a full 38-month,
month-by-month review, 2026-08-23 - 61 story files carry the direct
"per Alex's own account, month-by-month review, 2026-08-23" tag. This file
organizes that material by theme so the shape of the company is visible in
one read; every line below still points at its full story file, which holds
the threads, dates and exact wording. Read the story file before drafting
anything that depends on the specific detail, not just this summary

## How this file stays true

### The rebrand: "ChatGPT Central" to "AI Central" (name Dec 2024, identity Apr-Jun 2025)

Two separate events, easy to conflate. **The name itself** first appears on
the calendar 18 Dec 2024, with "MEET AI CENTRAL" on 6 Jan 2025 as the first
clean use with no "ChatGPT" trace. **The formal visual identity** - logo,
colors, the brand guidelines document - came later: Jorge first made contact
21 Feb 2025 (via Alessio, "Alessio x Jorge"), the relationship shifted to
Alex directly in April, and the actual brand identity project ran Apr-Jun
2025. Jorge's project gave the already-adopted name a formal identity: it
didn't create the name. Alex already believed "AI Central" would be the
eventual name even before this project - more useful and less politically
narrow than "ChatGPT Central," especially as other AI products entered the
market and tying the brand to one product (ChatGPT) became a limitation
rather than an asset. *Verified (calendar sweep) + per Alex - see
ai-central-brand-identity.md*
