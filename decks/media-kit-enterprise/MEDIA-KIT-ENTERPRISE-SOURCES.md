## Why this fork exists

Alex, 4 Sep 2026: the standard media kit and this edition go to different
audiences. This edition is for external stakeholders and enterprise sales,
where prices are either not the relevant question yet or the deal will be
bespoke-priced. The standard, priced kit stays as-is for transactional sales
(CMOs, GTM operators, influencer-marketing operators).

## What changed from the standard kit

- **Slides 1-6 (Cover, About, Why, Peace of mind, Audience, Publications):
  unchanged**, except the cover subtitle gains "· Enterprise Edition" and
  the footer reads "MEDIA KIT · ENTERPRISE" instead of "MEDIA KIT Q3 2026".
- **The four rate-card slides collapsed into one.** The standard kit's
  Advertising options overview (slide 7) plus three pricing deep-dives
  (Carousel, Main Ad, Email - slides 8-10) become a single slide 7, "Eight
  ways to reach them": all eight formats, same descriptions as the standard
  kit, laid out as an 8-tile grid with **no dollar figures, no discount
  table, no package tiers anywhere**. Alex confirmed this consolidation
  (rather than keeping four price-free slides) on 4 Sep 2026, via
  AskUserQuestion.
- **Premium formats slide (now slide 8, was 11): unchanged.** Already had
  no prices in the standard kit.
- **How it works (now slide 9, was 12): steps unchanged.** Only the closing
  "20% off 3+" discount-tier line is replaced with a "Custom bundles" line:
  bundles are quoted as one engagement, not sold off a list. The guarantee
  box's "Every bundle has agreed targets" is reworded to "Every engagement
  has agreed targets" for the same reason - "bundle" implied a fixed,
  priced package.
- **Case studies quick-glance (now slide 10, was 13): unchanged.** No
  dollar figures in the standard version either.
- **Team + contact (now slide 11, was 14): unchanged content**, except the
  "This kit" link changes from `cntral.ai/media-kit` to
  `cntral.ai/media-kit-enterprise`. **This URL is not live** - Alex needs to
  set up the redirect/page before this kit goes out, same as any other
  `cntral.ai/*` link referenced across these decks.

## Not changed

No client-facing numbers were added, removed, or rounded differently from
the standard kit outside of the rate-card consolidation above. No new
claims were introduced - every sentence in the new slide 7 already existed
in the standard kit's four rate-card slides, just recombined without the
price column.
