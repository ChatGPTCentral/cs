## Why this fork exists

Alex, 4 Sep 2026: pairs with the Enterprise media kit for the same
audience - external stakeholders and enterprise sales. Per AskUserQuestion,
the brief for "Extended" was "deeper narrative per client": headline and
objective copy rewritten to read as a fuller story per client, not just
added length elsewhere. Nothing else about the deck's data or structure
changes.

## What changed, per slide

- **Slides 1-2 (Cover, Renewals table): unchanged**, except the cover
  subtitle gains "· Extended Edition" and the footer reads "CASE STUDIES ·
  EXTENDED" instead of "CASE STUDIES 2026".
- **Outskill (slide 3).** Headline: "A Primary Ad slot Outskill kept in
  rotation for 16 straight months." Objective rewritten to state the full
  arc - a single placement in July 2024 becoming a standing slot across 25
  separate purchases through November 2025 - using only the placement count
  and date range already in the original.
- **Guidde (slide 4).** Headline: "Ten months of waves, timed to Guidde's
  own product calendar, not ours." Objective states the 21 placements
  between December 2024 and October 2025 ran in waves aligned to Guidde's
  own launch windows, not a fixed cadence - restating the original's
  campaign-type framing, not a new claim.
- **ElevenLabs (slide 5).** Headline: "A second campaign followed two
  months after the first had a full report." Objective adds that the first
  five-carousel batch (January 2026) had a complete report behind it before
  the second batch ran in March - both dates already in the original.
- **Luma AI (slide 6).** Headline: "A second campaign, four months later,
  once the first had proved out." Objective states the same: January's
  five carousels were reported on before the second five-carousel batch
  went into production in May. An earlier draft of this line claimed the
  four-month gap was "deliberate, not a rush to repeat a format" - dropped
  before publishing since no source states Luma's reasoning for the gap,
  only its length.
- **Gamma (slide 7).** Headline: "A second, larger batch, one month after
  the first." Objective states five carousels in January 2026 were followed
  by six in February, the second batch larger than the first - both counts
  already in the original.
- **Run yours (slide 8).** All "From $X" figures removed from the three
  format cards (Email placements, Bespoke carousels, Recurring waves). New
  line added: "Every engagement is scoped to your goals and quoted as one
  package - there is no rate card in this edition." The "media kit at" link
  changes from `cntral.ai/media-kit` to `cntral.ai/media-kit-enterprise` to
  match the Enterprise kit this deck pairs with.
